Our approach

Most clinics fit you into a seven-minute slot. We built Velune around the time good medicine actually takes: time to listen, to explain, and to plan properly.

How care works

Four steps. One doctor. No rushing.

  1. Listen
  2. Measure
  3. Plan
  4. Follow through
  1. 01

    Listen

    Your first consultation lasts forty-five minutes. Your doctor reads your history before you arrive, then asks about sleep, work, stress and family health as carefully as your symptoms.

    45 minutes for every first consultation

  2. 02

    Measure

    Blood tests, ECG, echo, ultrasound and genetic screening happen under one roof, usually in the same week. Samples are processed in our own accredited laboratory.

    80+ markers tested in-house

  3. 03

    Plan

    You receive a written care plan in plain language: what we found, what it means for you, and what happens next. Every number on your report is explained.

    72 hours from your tests to your written plan

  4. 04

    Follow through

    A named care coordinator books your follow-ups, sends reminders and answers questions on WhatsApp. You see the same lead doctor at every visit, year after year.

    One coordinator who knows your history

Questions

Before your first visit.

Can't find what you need? Call +91 484 000 2400 and a care coordinator will answer.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. We offer cashless billing with most major insurers and TPAs for eligible tests and procedures. For consultations, our billing desk prepares your reimbursement paperwork before you leave.

Do I need to fast before a health check?

For tests that include blood sugar and cholesterol, fast for 8 to 12 hours. Water is fine, and keep taking your regular medicines unless your doctor has told you otherwise.

Can I see a doctor by video?

Yes. Follow-ups and many first consultations work well by video. Choose Video when you book, and you'll receive a secure link by SMS fifteen minutes before your appointment.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring previous reports and prescriptions, a list of the medicines and supplements you take, and your insurance card if you have one. Please arrive ten minutes early to finish registration.

Can someone come with me?

Of course. A family member or friend is welcome in your consultation. For video visits, they can join from another phone using the same link.

How do I receive my reports?

Reports appear in the patient app and are emailed to you as a PDF. Your doctor walks you through them, in person or by video.
